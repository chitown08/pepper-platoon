Pepper Platoon

Your Garden

  • Leaves make ☀️ Sunlight and Roots draw 💧 Water, even while you're away. Tap them when a bubble pops up.
  • Sunlight plants and upgrades things. Water grows troops.
  • Blossoms house your platoon. Tap Platoon to grow troops.
  • Grow your Stem for a bigger plot, more storage and more buildings.
  • Plant a Flavor Lab and spend 🌱 Spice Seeds (earned from raid stars) on flavors that power up your troops.
  • Hit Raid! to attack a Bug Hive. Destroy its Compost Bin to unlock a tougher hive with better loot.

Sauce Raids

  • Deploy: pick a pepper below, then tap (or drag) on the red picnic blanket.
  • Jalapeños swarm the nearest building. Bell Peppers deal no damage but taunt nearby turrets. Habaneros lob explosive seeds over walls.
  • Heat builds over time and spikes when buildings fall. Spend 50 on a Hot Sauce Strike. It's great on walls.
  • Watch out for hidden Spicy Spores. Clumped troops get torched!
  • ★ 50% destroyed · ★ Compost Bin destroyed · ★ 100% destroyed
  • Troops you deploy are used up. Ones you don't deploy come home.
